One line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday — under 200
characters.

> My alarm clock rings at 6 AM on a Saturday like it has a court order, a megaphone, and a personal vendetta against my dreams.

## Why this line

The alarm is not a machine here, it is a petty official with paperwork: a
court order, a megaphone, and a vendetta. The punchline lands on the last
words — "vendetta against my dreams" — where *dreams* means both the sleep it
interrupted and the ambitions it is out to get. It is a fresh angle (the alarm
as a legal enforcer with a grudge), not the tired "I hate mornings" complaint,
and every word earns its place in one breath.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, no dependencies) writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), with a
self-check gate (1–200 characters, single line, not whitespace-only) and a
byte-exact read-back verification.
